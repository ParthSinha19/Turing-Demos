# AI & Machine Learning Labs

**Learn AI by building it, training it, and breaking it.**

A collection of interactive, browser-based AI and machine learning projects designed to make complex concepts accessible through hands-on experimentation. Built for classroom activities, educational demonstrations, and students exploring how intelligent systems work.

## Projects

### 1. Chatbot Lab

**Build Your Own Chatbot | Ages 9–11**

Create and customise a chatbot, teach it new information, and explore how conversational AI responds to different prompts.

* Runs in the browser using Transformers.js.
* Uses a lightweight language model running on the student's device.
* Includes interactive challenges, an AI detective activity, and reflection exercises.

[View Chatbot Lab](./Chatbot/)

### 2. Image Recognition and Regression Lab

**Train Your Own AI | Hands-on Machine Learning**

Explore image recognition and basic predictive modelling through interactive experiments. Train models using images of hands and fists, then experiment with a simple predictor to discover how data influences decisions.

* Experiment with training data and model predictions.
* Explore image recognition and regression concepts.
* Understand independent variables, target variables, and the importance of data quality.

[View Image Recognition and Regression Lab](./Image_Recognition_and_Regression/)

### 3. Recommendation Lab

**Can an Algorithm Learn Your Taste? | Ages 13–16**

Build a personalised movie feed by rating films and watching a content-based recommendation algorithm adapt to your preferences.

* Discover taste vectors and Euclidean distance.
* Add movies and compare recommendations with classmates.
* Explore personalisation, similarity, and the limitations of recommendation systems.

[View Recommendation Lab](./Recommender_System/)

## Learning Philosophy

These projects encourage students to learn by experimenting rather than simply observing. Students can modify data, challenge predictions, and see how their choices influence the results.

The goal is not to build perfect models, but to understand **how AI works, why it makes mistakes, and how humans can use it responsibly.**

## Getting Started

Each project has its own folder containing the relevant source files and documentation. Open the corresponding folder and follow its `README.md` for setup instructions and further details.

Most activities run directly in a modern web browser, with minimal setup required.

## Important Note

These projects are educational demonstrations, not production-grade AI systems. Some models and visualisations deliberately simplify real-world techniques to make underlying concepts easier to understand.
